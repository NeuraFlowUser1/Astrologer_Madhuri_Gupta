# Tall poster design plan

Design intent: make a light, textured and illustrated Sarsa poster feel like a phone page, with credible information, clear chapters and purposeful space. Preserve the existing lotus/star/kundli core and the supplied practitioner's identity. Keep A's successful composition and correct B/C's wasted side space in the contact section.

Implementation: native height/width 1.70–1.80, target standard 9:16. Use light cream, pale sage or linen paper with faint grain/washes and limited celestial/botanical corner studies. Muted olive-grey and matte brass supply readable foreground hierarchy and fine section rules. Keep the logo fully inside a richer ring. Place masthead horizontally near the top, introduction left and portrait right, then three service groups above two groups. Contact actions span one row; the phone has one row; email/address/site span three side-by-side cells.

Controlled planning ranges were 25–27% page width for the portrait, about 4.2–4.6% width for principal text, 2.5–2.7% for supporting copy and 18–21% width for scene studies. The generated artwork is reviewed as a complete composition; the planning guide is not assembled into the output. Exact native images are 941 x 1672, and link geometry follows their actual coordinates in manifest.json.

Review loop: inspect the earlier work and actual dimensions; review the canvas/layout plan twice; generate and measure the native image; inspect every element and all factual copy; preserve rejected attempts in numbered folders; generate the three current material/framing variations; review native and phone/desktop renderings; add invisible links; verify original bytes, embedded/rendered pixels, rectangles and destinations independently.

The original shorter 1122 x 1402 format was rejected. Round 06 had a valid 1.75 shape but needed layout correction. Round 07's 1.6203 shape failed the unchanged aspect requirement. Round 08 is the current native 1.7768 set. No failed attempt was stretched to pass.
